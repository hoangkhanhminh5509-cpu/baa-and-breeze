## 2. Series Bible & Viral Episode Formula

### Core Tagline
> *"The earth has music for those who listen."*

### Cast & Recurring World Elements
1. **Baa** — Curious cream-wool lamb with warm charcoal face & expressive listening ears. Speaks only in gentle musical *"Baa!"* bleats.
2. **Pip** — Baa's smaller friend lamb wearing a soft terracotta neck ribbon (`#CA5C3B`). Loves counting flowers and cheering with little hops.
3. **Breeze** — Visible pastel-blue swirling wind spirit that carries hidden melodies across the valley.
4. **Three Signature Habitats** (unified by one coastal valley map):
   - **Cottage & Singing Brook** (Water marimba, birdsong call-and-response)
   - **Golden Wheat Meadow** (3/4 wind waltz, 1–4 blooming wildflower rhythm)
   - **Lighthouse Cove & Orange Sailboat** (8-second ocean-tide breathing, brass harbor bell, dusk lullaby)

### The Viral Hook & Retention Architecture (8m 30s Master Format)
- **0:00 – 0:40 | Visual Hook & Mystery**: Opens on the iconic 3-panel postcard map. Breeze lifts a glowing golden note off the cottage porch and carries it toward the sea. Baa perks his ears (`♪ ?`) and sets out to find it. *(Zero loud dialogue -> instant curiosity across ALL languages!)*
- **0:40 – 2:40 | Interactive Call & Response (Brook)**: Water drops play marimba notes (`ListeningRipples` visual cues) -> Songbird sings 3-note ocarina phrase -> **2.5-second toddler pause** -> Baa echoes! Parents love watching kids tilt their heads and mimic *"Baa!"*.
- **2:40 – 5:10 | Montessori Counting & Rhythm (Golden Meadow)**: Breeze sweeps across tall yellow grass in 3/4 waltz; 4 wildflowers bloom sequentially (`1 - 2 - 3 - 4`) synced with overhead clouds and kalimba chimes.
- **5:10 – 7:20 | Emotional Co-Regulation (Lighthouse Cove)**: 8-second ocean wave inhale/exhale tide cycle calms hyper kids before naptime/bedtime while the orange sailboat rings its harbor bell.
- **7:20 – 8:30 | Musical Payoff & Bedtime Close**: All 3 collected Sound Bubbles harmonize together into an original lullaby under golden fireflies as the camera zooms back out to the 3-panel postcard.
